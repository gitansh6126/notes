# 📚 BDM Week 1 – Full Marks Exam Notes (Part 5) *(Based on your teacher's transcript – IITM Exam Oriented)* > **⭐ PYQ Importance: VERY HIGH** > > This lecture contains some of the most frequently asked BDM topics: > > * ⭐ B2B vs B2C > * ⭐ Product vs Service > * ⭐ IHIP Characteristics of Services > * ⭐ Pure Service vs Add-on Service > * ⭐ Reasons to Study Services > * ⭐ Decision Making Unit (DMU) > * ⭐ Jilting Impact --- # 1. B2C vs B2B ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Most Important) ## B2C (Business to Consumer) ### Definition Business sells directly to an **individual consumer**. ```text Business → Individual Customer ``` ### Example * Amazon → You * Nike → Customer * Reliance Fresh → Customer --- ## B2B (Business to Business) ### Definition Business sells to **another business**. ```text Business → Business ``` ### Example * Wholesaler → Retailer * Supplier → Amazon * Airbus → Air India --- ## Memory Trick **B2C = Business → Customer** **B2B = Business → Business** --- # 2. Product vs Service ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Product | Service | | ---------------- | ----------------- | | Tangible | Intangible | | Can be touched | Cannot be touched | | Ownership exists | No ownership | | Can be stored | Cannot be stored | --- ## Examples ### Product * Mobile * Pen * Laptop * Book --- ### Service * Banking * Education * Consultancy * Transport * Netflix --- # 3. IHIP Characteristics of Services ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ This is one of the highest probability theory questions. ## I = Intangible Cannot touch or see physically. Example Banking Teaching Insurance --- ## H = Heterogeneous Every service experience is different. Example Restaurant service today ≠ Restaurant service tomorrow. --- ## I = Inseparable Production and consumption happen together. Example Teacher teaches ↓ Students learn simultaneously. --- ## P = Perishable Cannot be stored. Example Missed Flight ↓ Cannot store today's flight for tomorrow. --- # Memory Trick Remember **IHIP** **I** Intangible **H** Heterogeneous **I** Inseparable **P** Perishable --- # 4. Why Services are Difficult to Evaluate? ⭐⭐⭐ Because they are **Intangible** Customers cannot: * Touch * Feel * Test before buying Therefore **Perceived Risk increases.** --- ### Memory Trick Service ↓ Invisible ↓ More Risk --- # 5. Types of Services ⭐⭐⭐ ## Pure Service Only services. Examples * Education * Banking * Consultancy --- ## Add-on Service Service provided with a product. Examples * Mobile warranty * Laptop servicing * Car servicing --- # Memory Trick Pure = Only Service Add-on = Product + Service --- # 6. Why Study Services? ⭐⭐⭐ Teacher gave three reasons. --- ## 1. GDP Contribution Services contribute a major part of GDP. Example * USA ≈ 80% * India ≈ 60% (approx. as discussed) --- ## 2. Employment Service sector creates many jobs. Examples * IT * Banking * Healthcare --- ## 3. Competitive Advantage Services help companies earn recurring revenue and stand out from competitors. Example Netflix subscription. --- # Memory Trick Remember **GEC** * **G** = GDP * **E** = Employment * **C** = Competitive Advantage --- # 7. B2B vs B2C Comparison ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | B2C | B2B | | -------------------------- | ------------------------ | | Business → Consumer | Business → Business | | End customer is Individual | End customer is Business | | Many Customers | Few Customers | | Long Supply Chain | Short Supply Chain | | Short Purchase Time | Long Purchase Time | | Lower Purchase Value | Very High Purchase Value | | Transactional Relationship | Long-term Relationship | | More Advertising | Less Advertising | | Simple Contracts | Detailed Contracts | | Individual Decision | DMU Decision | | Lower Jilting Impact | Higher Jilting Impact | --- # 8. Number of Customers ⭐⭐⭐ ### B2C Many customers. Example Shopping Mall --- ### B2B Few customers. Example Airbus Customers include: * Air India * Emirates * Qatar Airways --- # 9. Supply Chain ⭐⭐⭐ ## B2C Long Supply Chain ```text Manufacturer ↓ Distributor ↓ Wholesaler ↓ Retailer ↓ Customer ``` --- ## B2B Short Supply Chain Usually ```text Manufacturer ↓ Business Customer ``` or ```text Manufacturer ↓ Distributor ↓ Business Customer ``` --- # 10. Purchase Duration ⭐⭐⭐ ## B2C Decision Minutes Hours Days --- ## B2B Decision Months Sometimes One Year --- # Why? Purchase value is very high. --- # 11. Purchase Value ⭐⭐⭐ ### B2C Lower Example Shoes Mobile Watch --- ### B2B Very High Millions Billions Example Airplanes Industrial Machines --- # 12. Relationship ⭐⭐⭐ ## B2C Short-term Transactional Customers switch brands easily. --- ## B2B Long-term Collaborative Companies work together for years. --- # Memory Trick B2C Shopping B2B Partnership --- # 13. Price Elasticity ⭐⭐⭐ ## B2C Price Elastic Price ↑ ↓ Demand ↓ --- ## B2B Usually Price Inelastic Price ↑ ↓ Demand changes little (Though the teacher noted this can depend on the situation.) --- # 14. Advertisements ⭐⭐⭐ ## B2C Heavy Advertising Examples * Buy 1 Get 1 * Discounts * TV Ads --- ## B2B Very Less Advertising Focus is on direct business relationships. --- # 15. Agreements & Contracts ⭐⭐⭐ ### B2C Simple Small value --- ### B2B Detailed Contracts High value High risk --- # 16. Decision Maker ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ## B2C Individual Sometimes influenced by family. --- ## B2B Decision Making Unit (DMU) Also called Buying Center --- ## DMU Group of people from different departments. Example * Finance * HR * Engineering * Operations * Quality --- ### Memory Trick Big Purchase ↓ Big Team ↓ DMU --- # 17. Jilting Impact ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ## Definition The **shock or loss faced by a B2B company when a customer cancels after advanced negotiations**. --- ### B2C Lower Jilting Impact One customer leaves. Many others remain. --- ### B2B Higher Jilting Impact Few customers. Losing one customer causes a major impact. --- # Memory Trick Few Customers ↓ One leaves ↓ Big Shock = Jilting Impact --- # 🎯 Expected PYQs 1. Differentiate B2B and B2C. 2.
